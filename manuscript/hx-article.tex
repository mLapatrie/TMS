\subsection*{KiCAD schematic files}
\textbf{discharge.kicad\_sch} - Schematic overview of the entire TMS circuit with focus on the TMS discharge circuit. \\
\textbf{charge.kicad\_sch} - Main charging circuit schematic with measurements and control sub-circuits. \\
\textbf{gate\_driver.kicad\_sch} - Gate driver sub-circuit schematic. \\
\textbf{tms\_schematic.pdf} - Entire TMS circuit schematic in PDF format. \\

\subsection*{LTSpice schematic files}
\textbf{discharge.asc} - TMS inductor-capacitor oscillator discharge circuit schematic for SPICE simulation. \\
\textbf{charge.asc} - ZVS capacitor charging circuit schematic for SPICE simulation. \\
\textbf{gate\_driver.asc} - Gate driver circuit schematic for SPICE simulation. \\
\textbf{linear\_optocoupler.asc} - Linear optocoupler measurement circuit schematic for SPICE simulation. \\

\subsection*{FreeCAD parametric CAD files}
\textbf{TMS\_holder.FCStd} - Elevating table to hold the TMS coil for field measurements. \\
\textbf{sensing\_cross.FCStd} - Sensing cross to translate user movements to pick up coil movements. \\
\textbf{coil\_winder.FCStd} - Coil winder for winding the TMS coil. \\
\textbf{TMS\_field\_grid.pdf} - Measurement grid for optimal pick up coil positioning. \\

\subsection*{Control software files}
\textbf{controller\_arduino.ino} - Arduino sketch for controlling the TMS circuit. \\
\textbf{app.py} - Python script for the user interface. \\

\subsection*{Data collection and analysis files}
\textbf{collect\_grid.py} - Software interface with the oscilloscope for collecting pick up coil measurements. \\
\textbf{data\_analysis.ipynb} - Jupyter notebook for analyzing the data acquired with the TMS. \\
